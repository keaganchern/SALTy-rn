Interpretation:
  Confirmed ISA/C-intrinsic result: 1. The scalar shift register contains 32,
  and vssra.vx uses its low log2(SEW)=5 bits, so the effective e32 shift is 0.
  The pre-repair Lean helper used the raw Nat shift and evaluated this witness as
  0. `IntegerIntrinsics.lean` keeps an exact private copy of that historical helper
  and proves the old result is 0. The repaired production helper masks with
  `shift % 32` and the next regression proves the new result is 1. Both the test
  module and its updated rounding bridge build. This confirms that the old
  descriptor was a Lean modeling bug, not a difference in the target C program.
